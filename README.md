# AI + Business Course Project Gallery

A browsable record of student projects from AI/GenAI courses at the School of
Management and Economics, CUHK-Shenzhen — 66 completed team projects across three
academic years, plus 55 Fall 2026 projects still in progress, filterable by business
function, course and technology stack.

Completed projects are built from final project reports. The 2026-27 records
(`status: "in_progress"`) list topics only, taken from interim reports; their stack,
platform and artifact type are added after the final reports, and until then they
are left out of every stack, platform and artifact statistic. Student names, IDs and
contact details are excluded by design; partner organisations are generalised to an
industry label.

The home page also links this term's two student artifact galleries
(MIS3011 and IBA6316 · MDS5370).

A personal account of one instructor's teaching practice. SME offers a wider set
of AI-related courses beyond those represented here.
