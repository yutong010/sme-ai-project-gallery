# AI + Business Course Project Gallery

A browsable record of student projects from AI/GenAI courses at the School of
Management and Economics, CUHK-Shenzhen — 66 team projects across three academic
years, filterable by business function, course and technology stack.

Built from final project reports. Student names, IDs and contact details are
excluded by design; partner organisations are generalised to an industry label.

A personal account of one instructor's teaching practice. SME offers a wider set
of AI-related courses beyond those represented here.
